I pulled split-adjusted year-end closes for GOOG from Yahoo Finance (last trading day on or before 12/31 each year, starting in 2004 when Google went public). Price is in dollars per share. I used the same method as Phase 8: for each year-over-year period I calculated simple percent change for every factor, then weighted each one by its share of total movement that year (all weights add to 100%).

Over the full history (2001-2025), EPS is still the biggest mover and Shares is still the smallest; Price ranks 5th with about a 39% average move and roughly 22% of total movement. In the most recent five years (2020-2025), Price jumps to #1 at about a 53% average move, bigger than EPS. The main output files are phase9_seven_factor_panel.csv (percent change and weights by year), phase9_seven_factor_ranking_full_2001_2025.csv and phase9_seven_factor_ranking_recent5_2020_2025.csv (the two ranking tables), and data/goog_yahoo_year_end_prices.csv (the exact dates and prices used).
